About this demo

A concept demo built for the PM101 capstone (Group 6, Section G, Masters' Union), alongside the live product at nhe01.projectnhe.tech. Replies and poems are written fresh by an AI model through our own server - your name and words are sent for that, nothing else - and when the model is resting, she reads from her notebook instead.
