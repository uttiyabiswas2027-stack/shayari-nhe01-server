About this demo

A concept demo built for the PM101 capstone (Group 6, Section G, Masters' Union), alongside the live product at nhe01.projectnhe.tech. Poems are written fresh by an AI model through our own server - your name and mood are sent for that, nothing else - and when the model is resting, she reads from her notebook instead. She remembers only what stays in your own browser (localStorage) - nothing else leaves your device. Clear everything anytime:
